## ⚖️ Enforcement

These guidelines are meant to ensure consistency and quality across our projects. While we strive to follow them:
- They are living documents and can be updated based on team feedback
- Exceptions can be made when justified
- Focus on the spirit of the guidelines, not just the letter

## 🔄 Updating Guidelines

To propose changes to team guidelines:
1. Open an issue for discussion
2. Get consensus from the team
3. Submit a PR with the proposed changes
4. Update any affected documentation
